Released 2026-11-02. 12 updates since release, the newest 2026-11-09.

Newest update : Maps, Help and Cinematic (2026-11-09)

Z opens Cinematic Mode: every layer at once, WASD flies the focus, mouse orbits, wheel zooms

M maps for education, leisure, health and traffic (J K L ;), plus schools, hospitals and a Quality of Life panel

Race to 2040 lets you pick a 30K, 60K or 144K ark, with nine achievements to chase

A three-page Help guide pauses the ark while you read; elections are gone, your budget levers run the ark

Tax cuts boost economy health, solar makes half the power, and the hotkey bar sits in two rows

Earlier update : Five save slots (2026-11-02)

Your ark now has five manual save slots plus the Autosave.

Earlier update : Your Own Ark (2026-11-02)

Every player now gets their own ark, kept between visits.

Friends join your ark through Jayzee's Invite button.

Only an ark's owner can wipe it or start a new game. Joiners can't erase anything.

Each ark has its own four save slots, and the save list shows up as soon as you open Menu.

Earlier update : Oil by the barrel (2026-11-02)

The date panel now shows oil in $ per barrel under the $/MWh price. After 2040, oil doubles every 5 years.

Earlier update : Phone layout (2026-11-02)

The HUD now fits phones. Arrows scroll the top bar and the build palette, and the touch buttons stay clear.
