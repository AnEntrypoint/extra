### Pick a genre

Pick a genre

Where this toolbox shines brightest, from the strongest fit down. Open one for its whole case: the loop, the starter, the skills and clips it uses, real games built that way, and what the first build would be.

##### STRONGEST

read →

#### 3D action & adventure

A body in a 3D world with a sword, a gun or a spell, a place worth crossing and something in the way. Jayzee's strongest genre: start from 3d, add combat and juice.

read →

#### Multiplayer arenas & party games

Friends in one arena: rounds, a referee, a winner lit up at the end. Jayzee's rooms, invites and shared clock carry the networking; you build the rules and the fun.

read →

#### Exploration & atmosphere

A place worth wandering: light, weather, sound and secrets. The world is the game, and Jayzee's sky, terrain, trees and grass make it beautiful from the first hour.

read →

#### Racing & vehicles

Cars, karts, boats, planes, dragons: a dynamic hull you sit in, pushed by real forces. Add a track, checkpoints and a referee and you have a race.

read →

#### Survival & building

Gather, build, hold out through the night. Jayzee's block world digs and places out of the box; add hunger, a night that bites and a base worth defending.

##### VERY GOOD

read →

#### 2D platformers & top-down RPGs

Side-scrollers with jumps and parallax, top-down adventures with tiles, quests and loot: drawn heroes, fast loading and great on phones.

read →

#### Clickers, card & board games

Screen-first games where the rules matter most: tap the number up, play a hand, move a piece, solve today's puzzle, together with the room.

read →

#### Social hangouts

Places people come to be together: a plaza, café or club with seats, emotes, voice and chat already built in. The fun is the other people.

read →

#### NPC-driven story games

Stories carried by characters who answer what players say, remember each player, and decide with a mind of their own, chapter by chapter.

Tell me which one pulls you and I'll start building it.
