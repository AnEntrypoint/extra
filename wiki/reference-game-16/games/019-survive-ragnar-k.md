Ragnarok Online meets Vampire Survivors. The old-school MMO you grew up on, rebuilt as a horde survival hunt. Midgard is burning, and the monsters come in waves: hundreds at a time, wave after wave. Step out of Sanctuary with your friends, auto-fight through the swarm, then knock out the wild ones and catch them. Every catch becomes a companion that fights beside you, levels up and evolves. Pick your road as a Tamer, Ranger, Mystic, Guardian or Thief, take two job changes, and earn a God or Devil job. Push through eight lands, from the green Meadow and Ironwood Forest to the fires of Muspelheim, the ice of Frostmire, the gates of Hel, Alfheim, Myrkvid and golden Asgard. Drop the MVPs, crack their chests, chase rare shinies, and grab a soda from Fizzbit on the way out.
