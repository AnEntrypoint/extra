Newest update : Lane Cutters (2026-09-26)

Cars now switch lanes mid-drive to chase your dodges, forcing you to think two moves ahead.

Each prayer's starting traffic is noticeably faster — Fajr begins at 12 m/s instead of 7, climbing all the way to 38 m/s by Isha.

Goats have been cleared from Maghrib and Isha, making those prayers about pure car traffic only.

Earlier update : Five Streets, Five Prayers (2026-09-26)

Each prayer—Fajr to Isha—now runs on its own street, from the blue-and-white Medina at dawn through the harbour corniche and market garden to the lantern souk at night.

Hazards shift with the hour: slide under barriers at Asr, dodge goat herds at Maghrib, and weave through swerving cars on Isha's packed night street.

Your score is now your day: race all five prayers back-to-back and the leaderboard adds them up—a fresh day starts clean every morning.

New obstacles appear at each prayer: fruit-crate stacks, roadwork barriers, and a herd of goats that crosses the street sideways.

Earlier update : Mobile Ready (2026-09-25)

The game now plays beautifully on phones and tablets — all the clocks, timers, and prayer times scale perfectly to smaller screens.
