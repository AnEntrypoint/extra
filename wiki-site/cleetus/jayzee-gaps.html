Jayzee implementation gaps

How it works

docs/JAYZEE-GAPS.md (old name: savi) maps the documented cleetus featureset onto the rows of jayzee's PARITY.md (old name: jayzee for the platform, jayzee for the port). Its status words come from jayzee's committed docs/PARITY.md at commit 5fd6628c09384c1059af68839fb80e161f48c163 on origin main, not from the working tree.

The file states its rule. Feature completeness is the requirement, not architectural or operational parity. Any jayzee part may be idealized, so a missing jayzee path is not by itself a missing feature. The featureset documented in docs/FEATURESET.md (old name: savi) is a floor, not an exact specification. Where jayzee can build a part better than spoint, it should. A feature gap closes when a working jayzee module with a node-check witness covers it, whatever the path. The gate fails on any new missing row, and requires a witness for every removal from the open set.

Of the 67 rows here, 65 map to jayzee's ranked rows at that pin: rows 2 to 40 map to 1 to 39, rows 42 to 60 map to 40 to 58, row 61 maps to 59, row 1 maps to 60, and rows 62 to 66 map to 61 to 65. Rows 41 and 67 map to U41 and U67 under "Not in spoint". Rows 13 and 36 are unverified as closed, because jayzee's PARITY.md still says partial for both at that pin. Row 36 no longer lists the host GC observer as open, since jayzee ports it as src/telemetry-pure/gcObserver.js.

Tags: required-in-jayzee means jayzee's PARITY.md marks the row partial or open, and the quoted open clause is the implementation queue. None (built) means jayzee marks it built, and no gap is recorded.

scripts/check-ideal-coverage.mjs (old name: savi) reads the numbered rows of this file and requires each to be named in ../jayzee/docs/IDEAL.md. manual-build.md describes it.

Decisions

  • Feature completeness, not architectural parity, is the bar. A missing jayzee path is not a missing feature, and a feature gap closes on a working module with a node-check witness.
  • Status words come from jayzee's committed PARITY.md at the pin, not from the working tree, so the register reflects a fixed commit, and the mapping of rows is explicit.
  • Rows whose status is unverified as closed (13 and 36) are kept open here until jayzee's PARITY.md changes.
  • Where jayzee improves on spoint (row 14: validating consecutiveDesyncsRequired; row 6 and row 33: the idealized limiter), the gap is closed in jayzee's favour and the spoint behaviour is not reproduced.
  • The gate fails on any new missing row, so the open set cannot grow silently.
