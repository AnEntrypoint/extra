Featureset part 2: streams 12 to 25 and the src/sdk modules

How it works

This page covers lines 131 to 360 of docs/FEATURESET.md (old name: savi). Each stream is a read-only survey of the spoint tree (/workspace/spoint, read-only at commit d5e3860). Each line gives a path and line number, the snippet found there, and, where a feature has no consumer, the negative grep that found none. The stream sections were produced in the same pass as the text that cites them, and the file states that nothing was written into spoint. Jayzee rows are read from docs/PARITY.md at jayzee 2db34a0 for stream 12, and from 17ce428 for stream 11 (see featureset-part-1.md).

The rows cited here are in jayzee-gaps.md and netcode.md.

Migration inside the worker: none. No match for MIGRATE, 0xb7 or 0xb8 in src/transport or WorkerEntry.js, and no match for TransportMigrationTrigger or migrateTransport there. The worker is not on the migration path.

  • Every line is re-read at the pin before a section is written, so the citations match d5e3860 rather than the working tree.
